# Coverboard validation sprint — decide before you shelve

Goal: in ~3–4 weeks, get an honest **continue / narrow-pivot / shelve** decision,
without writing more code. The product already exists, so you can do better than
interviews — you can watch people *try* it.

## The one question this sprint answers

> For UK shift businesses, is there a real, reachable segment whose current rota/
> leave tool **fails them on variable-hours holiday or cover** — painfully enough
> that they'd switch to a dedicated tool?

The competitive reality to disprove: RotaCloud, FlowRota and care systems (Log my
Care, Florence, Birdie) already do 12.07% holiday + leave limits, rota-native, at
low/no-contract pricing. So the bar is high. That's the point.

## Set the kill criterion NOW (before you talk to anyone)

Decide the bar up front so you can't rationalise later. Target **12–15
conversations** with real operators. Then:

- **CONTINUE** if **≥4** describe a *current, specific, painful* gap in how their
  existing tool handles variable-hours holiday OR cover **AND ≥2** give a real
  commitment (try it on their data / a scheduled follow-up / an intro / "tell me
  when I can pay").
- **NARROW-PIVOT** if the pain is real but it's a *different* problem than you
  built (follow that problem).
- **SHELVE** if most say "our rota tool / accountant handles holiday & cover
  fine," or the pain is mild. No shame — you'll have saved months.

## Who to talk to

The buyer (not the employee): **ops manager / registered manager / owner** at
small UK shift businesses — care homes & domiciliary care (best fit), independent
hospitality, pharmacies, dental/vet practices. ~5–50 staff, has zero-hours/bank
staff.

## How to reach them (async, cold-start)

You need ~30–40 touches to land 12–15 conversations. Channels:
- **LinkedIn**: search "registered manager" / "care home manager" / "operations
  manager hospitality" UK → short, no-pitch message (below).
- **Niche communities** (see `distribution-targets.md`): care/registered-manager
  FB groups, AccountingWeb, r/CareUK.
- **Local + direct**: care homes / pubs / pharmacies via Google Maps → email the
  manager.
- **Door-opener**: lead with the free calculator, not the app.

**Outreach opener (value-first, no pitch):**
> "Hi [name] — I'm researching how small [care/hospitality] businesses handle
> holiday and cover for shift and zero-hours staff (building a free tool in the
> space). Could I ask you 4–5 quick questions about how you do it today? 15
> minutes, genuinely just learning — no pitch. Happy to share the free
> variable-hours holiday calculator either way."

## The interview script (Mom Test rules)

**Rules:** ask about their *past behaviour and real problems*, never about your
idea or the future. "Would you use it?" and "do you like it?" are worthless. Talk
less; let them talk. Dig into stories.

1. "How do you handle staff time off / rotas today — what tool or system?"
2. "When you take on a zero-hours or bank worker, how do you work out their
   holiday? Does the tool do it, or you/your accountant?"
3. "Tell me about the **last time** approving leave caused a staffing headache."
4. "Has holiday pay for variable-hours staff ever been a worry — an underpayment,
   a complaint, an audit, an accountant fixing it?"
5. "What's the most annoying thing about how you handle time off right now?"
6. "Have you ever gone looking for a better tool? What happened — what stopped
   you, or why did you switch?"
7. (Only after listening) "Could I show you something in a couple of weeks and
   get your reaction?" ← soft commitment test.

The decisive answers are to #1, #2, #3, #6: if their rota tool already does
holiday + cover and they're fine with it → that's your shelve signal, repeated.

## Better than interviews: watch them use it

Because Coverboard exists, get 3–5 willing operators to **try it on their real
data** (or screen-share while you watch). Usage beats opinions. Watch for: do they
get to the "aha" (book a zero-hours leave → correct accrual; a cover warning)
without you steering? Where do they stall? Would they keep using it next week?

## What counts as a real signal (commitment, not compliments)

Rank every conversation:
- ❌ Noise: "looks great," "interesting," "good luck," "send me info" (then silence).
- ⚠️ Mild: agrees the problem exists but shrugs / offloads to accountant.
- ✅ Real: shows you their current spreadsheet/tool unprompted · books a follow-up
  · tries it on real data · asks "when can I have it / what's it cost" · intros a
  peer · offers to pilot or pre-pay.

Count the ✅s against the kill criterion. Compliments are not validation.

## Timeline & cadence (~4–6 hrs/week)

- **Week 1**: build the list (30–40 names), send openers, set up the free-calc
  door-opener. Book first calls.
- **Weeks 2–3**: 12–15 conversations + 3–5 hands-on try-outs. Log each (problem?
  current tool? commitment level?).
- **Week 4**: tally ✅s vs the kill criterion. Make the call. Write it down.

## The decision

Score honestly against the criterion you set on day one. CONTINUE → you've found
the wedge; build/market hard on the *specific* pain they named. NARROW-PIVOT →
chase the real problem. SHELVE → you proved it cheaply; redeploy the months you'd
have spent. All three are wins versus building on a guess.
